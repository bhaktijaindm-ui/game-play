## 3. Long Description

Step into the world of high fashion, beauty, and dazzling puzzle thrills with **Glam Blocks** — the ultimate cosmetic-themed block puzzle game!

Indulge in a relaxing, satisfying puzzle experience crafted for beauty lovers. Drag velvet lipsticks, shimmer eyeshadow palettes, glowing highlighters, and precision eyeliners onto the 8x8 grid. Complete horizontal and vertical rows to trigger sparkling clears, massive combo cascades, and high-score multipliers!

### ✨ KEY FEATURES:

💄 **GLAMOROUS MAKEUP THEME**
No boring shapes here! Every puzzle block is an iconic beauty essential — satin lipsticks, chromatic eyeshadows, peach blush swirls, lustrous nail polishes, 24K gold highlighters, and cooling mint skincare serums.

👑 **AVATAR & LOOKS STUDIO (META LAYER)**
Your puzzle score is your ticket to beauty stardom! Level up and score big to unlock exquisite makeup looks for your in-game avatar. Collect stunning styles like *Natural Glow*, *Berry Chic*, *Sunset Shimmer*, *Cyber Glam*, *Gothic Velvet*, and the regal *Royal Empress*. Mix, match, and show off your style!

💥 **POWERFUL BEAUTY BOOSTERS**
Conquer tricky boards with salon-grade power-ups:
- **Undo**: Second thoughts? Reverse your previous move.
- **Shuffle**: Roll three brand-new cosmetic shapes adhering to our guaranteed fairness rule.
- **Glam Bomb**: Clear a full 3x3 blast area with radiant sparkles!

🏆 **WEEKLY HALL OF GLAM (GLOBAL LEADERBOARD)**
Compete against top puzzle stylists worldwide! Climb the weekly Top 50 global leaderboard, earn golden crowns, and achieve personal high score records.

🎁 **DAILY STREAK REWARDS**
Check in every single day to open your 7-day streak vanity calendar and collect free coins, sparkling gems, and exclusive boosters.

📶 **100% OFFLINE PLAYABLE & BATTERY FRIENDLY**
No Wi-Fi? No problem! Glam Blocks works completely offline with instant auto-save so you can resume your game anytime on planes, subways, or during quick coffee breaks. Built with a featherlight engine that runs at a silky-smooth 60fps on all Android devices.

🌟 **FAIR & RELAXING GAMEPLAY**
No timers, no fake urgency, and no frustrating impossible drops. Our smart fairness engine ensures a legal placement exists on the board.

Download **Glam Blocks** today and transform every puzzle move into pure glamour!
