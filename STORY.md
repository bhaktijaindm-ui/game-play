# Glam Blocks: The Gulab Mahal Salon — Story & Character Bible

## Setting
**Rangpur**: A vibrant, sun-drenched town nestled between rolling hills and marigold fields in India. Famed for its lively bazaars, heritage courtyards, and bustling festival streets.
At the corner of Bazaar Lane stands **Gulab Mahal Salon** — once the crown jewel of Rangpur where grandmother ("Nani") Sharda Sharma created signature makeup artistry for generations of brides and townspeople. After sitting closed for three years, 26-year-old Anaya Sharma arrives with her grandmother's legacy and a determination to restore every room to its former splendor.

---

## Core Characters

### 1. Anaya Sharma (Protagonist & Salon Owner, Age 26)
- **Appearance**: Long wavy dark-brown hair with a signature magenta streak, kind hazel eyes, rose-pink salon apron over a black tee, polished gold bangles that clink softly as she works.
- **Personality**: Warm, resilient, deeply creative, attentive listener. Believes makeup isn't a mask—it's celebration and self-expression.
- **Role**: Gameplay guide, restores the salon room by room, paints customer faces, deciphers Nani's Rang Book.

### 2. Zoya Akhtar (Best Friend & Nail Artist, Age 25)
- **Appearance**: Short bouncy curly hair, mint-green linen co-ord set, colorful rings on every finger, bright coral sneakers.
- **Personality**: Bubbly, fiercely loyal, trendsetter, energetic comic relief who loves dramatic hand gestures.
- **Role**: Manages the Nail Bar, introduces daily tasks and customer order challenges.

### 3. Kabir Sen (Handyman & Childhood Friend, Age 27)
- **Appearance**: Round wire glasses, rolled-up denim jacket, heavy-duty leather tool belt, dusty work boots, warm crooked grin.
- **Personality**: Cheerful, reliable, inventive craftsman who can fix antique vanity lights with paperclips and ingenuity.
- **Role**: Handles salon room renovations, structural repairs, and furniture assembly.

### 4. Tara-ji (Mentor & Veteran Makeup Artist, Age 62)
- **Appearance**: Impeccable silver bob, crisp handloom cotton sarees with silver borders, oversized reading glasses, serene poise.
- **Personality**: Wise, affectionate, dry wit, culinary storyteller. Was Nani's closest apprentice in the 1980s.
- **Role**: Mentors Anaya on classic blending techniques and helps interpret the mysterious riddles in Nani's Rang Book.

### 5. Kajal (Salon Mascot & Lucky Black Cat)
- **Appearance**: Sleek velvet-black fur, amber eyes, tiny gold bell collar that jingles playfully.
- **Personality**: Curious, regal, nap connoisseur, loves sleeping on empty velvet chairs and batting at loose cosmetic brushes.
- **Role**: Gives random bonus coins, appears during combo streaks, accompanies Anaya across the salon rooms.

### 6. Rhea Mehra (Friendly Rival turned Collaborative Partner, Age 28)
- **Appearance**: Razor-sharp angled bob, impeccably tailored pastel blazers, pearl studs, crisp tablet stylus always in hand.
- **Personality**: Ambitious, polished, competitive perfectionist who initially thinks local traditional salons are obsolete, but gradually discovers the warmth of community artistry.
- **Role**: Owner of the "Lumiere" beauty chain at the Grand Mall; challenges Anaya in friendly seasonal beauty competitions before partnering for the town fair.

## Nani's "Rang Book" — The 7 Secret Signature Looks

1. **Look 1: Gulab Dawn (Chapter 1 Unlock)**
   - *Lore*: Nani's first bridal look from 1974. Fresh morning dew over garden rose petals.
   - *Key Shades*: Rose Pink + Champagne Glow.
   - *Zone Focus*: Dewy cheeks and rosebud lips.

2. **Look 2: Rangpur Radiance (Chapter 1 Unlock)**
   - *Lore*: Created for the town's annual spring heritage parade. Bright and optimistic.
   - *Key Shades*: Peach Blush + Gold Shimmer.
   - *Zone Focus*: Luminous cheekbones and soft gold eye wash.

3. **Look 3: Mehendi Glow (Chapter 2 Unlock)**
   - *Lore*: Crafted to complement fresh henna and evening fairy lights without fading.
   - *Key Shades*: Mint Fresh + Warm Amber.
   - *Zone Focus*: Bright under-eyes, bronze contour, and terracotta lips.

4. **Look 4: Sangeet Sparkle (Chapter 2 Unlock)**
   - *Lore*: Designed to catch stage spotlights during night-long musical celebrations.
   - *Key Shades*: Lavender Foil + Ruby Shine.
   - *Zone Focus*: Ultra-sparkling eyelids and high-pigment gloss.

5. **Look 5: Holi Bloom (Chapter 3 Unlock)**
   - *Lore*: Celebrating the vibrancy of spring powders with gentle botanical pigments.
   - *Key Shades*: Sky Blue + Coral Flush.
   - *Zone Focus*: Gradient pastel eye shadow and flush gradient lips.

6. **Look 6: Diwali Gold (Chapter 3 Unlock)**
   - *Lore*: Inspired by flickering earthen diyas and regal zari embroidery.
   - *Key Shades*: Antique Gold + Deep Maroon.
   - *Zone Focus*: Gilded temple lids and velvet bold lips.

7. **Look 7: Royal Gulab Bride (Chapter 4 Grand Finale Unlock)**
   - *Lore*: Nani Sharda's crowning masterpiece. Balanced, timeless, majestic grace.
   - *Key Shades*: Pure Crimson + Golden Dust + Champagne Highlight.
   - *Zone Focus*: Flawless full face harmony across Eyes, Cheeks, and Lips.
